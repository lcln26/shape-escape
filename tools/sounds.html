Sound check

Every sound in the game, playable on its own. The first click turns audio on. If something sounds off, say which button and what's wrong ("marimba too clicky", "music too quiet", "streak sounds random").

Click anything to start audio.

Shape voices

Each shape sings with its own voice when you catch it. Notes are from one five-note scale, A4 up to D6.

Music

The soundtrack at each stage of a run. Turn on "catches" to hear simulated catches on the beat, the way they'd sound mid-run.
